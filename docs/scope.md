# FairWork — Project Scope

## Overview

FairWork is an AI-powered platform that helps freelancers and clients establish clear agreements, manage deliverables, detect scope changes, review evidence, resolve misunderstandings, and handle milestone payments through PayPal.

## Problem

Freelancers often experience disagreements over project requirements, revisions, and payments because agreements are unclear or change over time.

FairWork helps prevent these problems by maintaining a shared, traceable record of commitments, work completed, and changes approved by both parties.

## Target Users

- Freelance software developers
- Graphic designers
- Video editors
- Writers and other digital freelancers
- Individuals and businesses hiring freelancers

## Core Features

1. Freelancer and client accounts.
2. AI agreement generation.
3. Shared agreement approval.
4. Milestone and project management.
5. AI scope-change detection.
6. AI deliverable and evidence review.
7. Change requests and amendments.
8. Revision management.
9. PayPal sandbox invoicing and payments.
10. Dispute and evidence workspace.
11. Notifications, reporting, and project history.

## AI Principles

- AI recommendations must reference agreement requirements or submitted evidence.
- AI must communicate uncertainty and cannot invent evidence.
- Both parties approve material agreement changes.
- AI cannot independently authorize payments or make legally binding decisions.

## Main Demo Scenario

A freelance developer agrees to build a portfolio website. The client later requests an additional booking system.

FairWork identifies the new requirement as a potential scope change, compares it to the approved agreement, and helps both parties create a separate milestone.

Once the client approves the completed work, FairWork generates a PayPal sandbox invoice and records the payment status.
